## Design Context

### Users

Hostel Premium is for UCC students looking for hostel or accommodation options before expanding to other Ghanaian universities. Students may arrive from Google, shared links, or social channels and need to quickly search, compare, save, and send enquiries without confusion.

Secondary users include hostel managers, owners, admins, support users, parents, and future mobile app users. Manager content must remain accessible but secondary to student discovery on the homepage.

### Brand Personality

The interface should feel premium, trustworthy, student-friendly, organized, calm, and local to UCC/Ghana. It must be serious enough for students, parents, managers, and authorities.

It should not feel cheap, childish, generic SaaS, luxury hotel, food delivery, random real estate, or AI-generated.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a clean accommodation-marketplace language:

```text
White canvas
Ink black text
Soft gray structure
Restrained rose primary action
Real hostel/student accommodation imagery
Search-first marketplace rhythm
```

References are for structure only:

```text
Airbnb / booking-style marketplaces: search prominence, image-led browsing, filters, map/list behavior.
Expedia: search module clarity, category tabs, serious search flow.
Amber Student: student accommodation hierarchy and trust signals.
Reales UI Kit: polished property-card rhythm and component spacing.
```

Do not copy brand identity, exact layouts, proprietary assets, protected copy, or unsupported trust claims.

### Design Principles

1. Search first. The homepage first screen should make UCC hostel discovery obvious and keep search visible immediately.
2. Use fewer words above the fold. Let search controls and hostel imagery carry the first impression.
3. Keep trust precise. Use exact labels such as Owner Submitted, Admin Reviewed, Photos Confirmed, and Physically Verified only when truthful.
4. Do not stretch mobile into desktop. Desktop needs real marketplace density; mobile needs focused, thumb-friendly flow.
5. Manager paths are secondary. They should be findable without competing with student search.
6. Use approved colors only. Avoid cream/gold themes, purple gradients, generic blue SaaS, and noisy decoration.
7. Images must be honest. Temporary stock images are placeholders only and must not imply real verified UCC hostel photos.
