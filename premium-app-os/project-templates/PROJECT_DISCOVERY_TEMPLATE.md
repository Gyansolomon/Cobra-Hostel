## Acceptance Criteria

The project is ready to plan when:

- The MVP is clear.
- The target users are clear.
- The main pages/screens are listed.
- The stack recommendation is selected.
- Design direction is approved.
- Paid tools and setup needs are disclosed.
- Roadmap phases are approved.
